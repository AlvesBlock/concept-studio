# Roadmap

- v0.1: Foundation / initial canvas.
- v0.2: Generic document model, built-in diagram registry and graph adapter.
- v0.3: Interactive concept maps.
- v0.4: Automatic layout.
- v0.5: Local document management and IndexedDB.
- v0.6: Import/export and CXL interoperability.
- v0.7: Mind maps.
- v0.8: Workspaces and thematic rooms.
- v0.9: Beta quality, accessibility and documentation.
- v1.0: Stable local-first release.
- Later: optional sync, collaboration, AI and additional diagram types.

Versions are targets, not release promises.
